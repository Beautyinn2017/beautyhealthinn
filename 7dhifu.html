Advanced HIFU + RF Technology

NEW DOUBLO 7D HIFU delivers focused ultrasound and RF energy to both superficial and deeper layers of the skin to support lifting, tightening and rejuvenation with minimal downtime.

  • 1.5mm face handle – wrinkles, pores and eye area refinement
  • 2.0mm face handle – mild skin laxity and cheek support
  • 3.0mm face handle – jawline definition and contour support
  • 4.5mm face handle – deeper SMAS layer support and V-line contouring
  • 6mm body handle – body contouring and skin firmness support
  • 9mm body handle – body tightening and contour enhancement
